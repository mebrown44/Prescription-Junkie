A More Approachable Approach

It doesn't matter the reason: sometimes transferring prescriptions sucks. We don't want to make phone calls, we don't want to have to find a place that accepts our insurance, or we're simply too overwhelmed to pick up another task. Prescription is a better, easier approach for anyone.

With Accessibility in Mind

If you have mobility, hearing, or sight impairments, there's more to consider about picking a pharmacy than just which one is the closest. If you have less common medications, it's good to get an idea of where might have them before having to do all of the research yourself. Maybe you're HoH and want to know if a pharmacist knows ASL or maybe you're an elder who isn't very confident using technology: this was made for you.
